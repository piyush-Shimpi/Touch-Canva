## G. Honesty note
- **Mixamo characters have no facial expressions.** Fear and shock come from body animation (looking back, flinching). This is the biggest limit of the look.
- The crowd is the same 2 zombies repeated with different timing, scale and colour.
- The city and mountains are stylised 3D blockouts, not photoreal.
- The map is a stylised visual of the study's result, not the actual simulation data. "Month 4" is an estimate; the study says "months".
- The study's authors also said that with realistic settings "we are largely doomed." The script says "last longest", not "survive forever".
- Rendering takes about 2 hours on this machine.
